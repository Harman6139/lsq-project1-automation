Assignment 10: closest literature and contribution, 19 November 2026

The method alone is not new. Return-screen combinations, detection-versus-warning curves, investigation budgets and validation already have close precedents. The possible increment is evidence that a fixed rule remains useful across different dates, strategies and reporting mechanisms, including genuinely high-performing histories.

Closest comparison | What exists already | What we must add
Bollen–Pool 2008 | Loss-delay model; false warnings, missed detection and history length | Transfer beyond a single reporting mechanism.
Bollen–Pool 2012 | Multiple return flags, probit fraud-risk score and warning-budget comparisons | New controlled evidence; no claim to invent combined screens.
Joenväärä–Kosowski 2015 | UCITS comparison; aggregated warnings and principal component | Frozen, separately validated choices. Exact PCA normalization is unspecified.
Jorion–Schwarz 2014 | Fees and legitimate economics can create zero-return discontinuities | Explicit return definitions and restrained attribution.
van Vuuren and van Vuuren 2022 | Bias ratio alongside return moments, indices and known cases | A disciplined common-budget comparison.
González–Papageorgiou–Skinner 2016 | Top-performing fund selection, strategy groups, later diagnostic persistence | New operating tradeoffs, not the first strong-performer analysis.
Dimmock–Gerken 2012 | Fraud prediction and investigation budgets with out-of-sample checks | Evidence on what returns alone can support.
Beneish–Vorst 2022 | Seven-model error-cost and investigation-capacity comparison | Fund-return evidence with clearly assumed costs/prevalence.
Bao et al.2020/2022 | Ensemble comparison; correction for training/test overlap | Keep strategy identities and altered versions together.
Chowdhry–Saxena 2026 | Downside-variation diagnostics; skewness-sensitive null | Delimit the four chosen families. Latest revision differs from downloaded version.

What would make the contribution convincing
At a fixed investigation budget, a rule chosen beforehand should find materially more imposed reporting problems on genuinely separate test data. Show whether that advantage survives a new strategy family and a different reporting policy. A higher average hit rate on familiar transformations is insufficient. If no rule transfers reliably, report the particular failures rather than claim returns cannot help.

Economic limits
These are reconstructed portfolios and controlled reporting alterations, not verified honest/fraudulent manager histories. They cannot estimate fraud prevalence, real classification precision or monetary losses avoided. A high diagnostic ratio caused by cash interest is not the same as Fund X-like investment performance. The final-data frontier is exploratory if it selects the best rule after seeing those data.

Risk Sciences
The subject is within the journal’s published scope. Its research-article guide asks for substantial advances. Boudt, Delmarcelle and Ringoot’s 2025 AML alert paper connects a system to supervisors’ actual workflow and examines real events; Eling and Jung’s cyber-risk paper links a stated economic mechanism to a decision. Our study needs an equally clear decision and supported increment. This comparison does not establish acceptance prospects.
