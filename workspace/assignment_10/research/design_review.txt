The objective is to compare additional responses to specified reporting changes at a fixed prior-reference warning budget, then test whether a small selected rule set remains preferable across the declared settings. A frontier is an established diagnostic. A consequential finding about rule complementarity, robustness or collapse within a supported exceptional-return range would have to supply the contribution.

The four families can be the existing bias ratio, fitted-normal loss deficit, positive lag-one sample autocorrelation and a predeclared local zero score. The fourth score needs special care because the probability of a small gain within a finite symmetric band need not be one half under an ordinary smooth distribution. A fixed local contrast using adjacent inner and outer bands can cancel a smooth linear density slope. With b equal to one quarter of sample standard deviation, let L2, L1, G1 and G2 be counts in [-2b,-b), [-b,0), (0,b] and (b,2b]. The score [3(G1-L1)-(G2-L2)]/sqrt[9(G1+L1)+(G2+L2)] is one transparent choice, set to zero when the denominator vanishes. Count exact zeros separately. This is an empirically calibrated score, not a Bollen-Pool p-value. A simpler local sign-imbalance score is acceptable if labelled accordingly. Freeze the choice and bandwidth before examining outcomes.

Freeze historical percentile transformations for the four scores. OR uses the largest percentile in a subset. Strict majority of q rules uses the floor(q/2)+1-th largest percentile. There are 26 distinct rules after removing four duplicated singleton OR/majority rules. Each full combination needs its own calibration. Reusing individually calibrated component cutoffs would give combinations unequal warning budgets.

The decisive findings are falsifiable. A small selected rule set may improve held-out response across settings, mechanisms may require different rules, selecting winners may change the relation in either direction, or no stable improvement may survive. The number and type of preferred rules remain conditional on the four families, warning budget, historical reference and reporting alternatives. The present data support an empirical strategy-benchmark study, while real-fund fraud accuracy still requires independently established outcomes.
